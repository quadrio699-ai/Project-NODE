Having discussed the concept and functions of management, it is important that we look at Principles of Management. 

Principle entails a scientific theory or fundamental truth or proposition that guides the actions of men and things. 

Management principles are the fundamental propositions, facts and truths on how management should be carried out. 

In other words, principles of management signify the regulations and laws that guide the practice of management. 

The most notable of all management principles is Fayol's 14 Principles of Management, which include: 

1. Division of Work 

2. Authority 

3. Discipline 

4. Unity of Command 

5. Unity of Direction 

6. Subordination of Individual Interests to the General Interest 

7. Remuneration 

8. Centralisation 

9. Scalar Chain 

10. Order 

11. Equity 

12. Stability of Tenure of Personnel 

13. Initiative 

14. Esprit de Corps 

Having understood the underpinning of Management, it is important that we apply it to Education. This is known as Educational Management. 

Educational Management is the process of planning, organising, directing and controlling the activities of an institution by utilising human, financial and material resources so as to effectively and efficiently accomplish functions of teaching, community services and research. 

Educational management can also be seen as the artistic, systematic, and scientific application of management principles to the judicious use of available resources towards the effective and efficient actualisation of educational objectives, aims or goals in an educational setting. 

# **Aims of Educational Management include:** 

To achieve educational institution objectives 

Improve processes of planning, organising and implementing educational policies and programmes to achieve educational goals 

To ensure optimal utilisation of human resources (administrators, teaching and non-teaching staff, including students) 

To enable teacher job satisfaction and productivity 

To build relationships with immediate and extended community 

To improve interpersonal communication 

To produce needed manpower and enhance human capital formation 

# **Functions of Educational Management** 

1. Planning of educational activities and goals 

2. Organising educational structures and creating organogram 

3. Budgeting educational income and expenditures 

4. Directing educational affairs 

5. Controlling educational operations through evaluation, reports and monitoring 

6. Ensuring quality assurance

# **<u>Concept of Educational Administration</u>** 

Educational administration as a discipline or profession was borrowed from the concept of administration but applied to educational matters. When compared to other fields such as Philosophy, Theology, Medicine, Public Administration, it may be said to be a relatively new discipline. 

More specifically, educational administration is a subset of educational management, and so it cannot be totally divorced from all the attributes of educational management. 

Educational administration is a service activity or tool through which the major objectives of education can be achieved. It involves the activities of educational organisations directed towards the accomplishment of teaching–learning goals. Agencies that are directly in charge of school administration include the Ministry of Education, the Teaching Service Commission, the Local Education Districts and similar bodies. 

# **_Objectives of Educational Administration_** 

1. Providing education to students – ensuring qualitative improvement of education, qualified teachers, and student success. 

2. Ensuring utilisation of resources – proper use of finances, materials, textbooks, programmes, and extracurricular activities. 

3. Measuring student performance – regular assessments by teachers and staff. 

4. Promoting students’ success – effective hiring to match the right teachers with the right classes. 

5. Hiring qualified teachers – improving students’ understanding and learning speed. 

6. To actualise the national policy on education. 

# **Functions of Educational Administration** 

1. Financial decision-making 

2. Staff supervision and assessment 

3. Curriculum design 

4. Establishment of instructional procedures 

5. Student services 

6. Personnel management and administration
