il  **GENERAL CONCEPT AND PRINCIPLE OF FIRST AID**  There is increased competition in every sphere of life, and that portends increased exposure to sudden health problems. Hence, the need for more active first aid knowledge and practice. The occurrence of health hazards and emergencies, either at home, on campus or place of work is usually unplanned and sudden and can deteriorate and lead to loss of life, therefore, emergency situation requires immediate attention. 

 The type of attention given to health emergencies is universally referred to as **FIRST AID.** First Aid refers to the immediate attention or assistance given to an injured or ill person before professional medical treatment can be provided. First Aid aims to preserve life, prevent the condition from worsening, and promote recovery. First Aid treatment can be given by anyone who has learnt the principles and knows what to do in case of an emergency. First Aid entails certain principles and skills that must be known before it can be appropriately administered. It may involve the use of available or improvised materials, so far it is within an acceptable guide. 

1 

il  Two important keywords in First Aid are: 

 **Immediate:** this means that First Aid is the prompt attention given on the spot of the health emergency, injury or sudden illness to prevent complications and to save life. 

 **Temporary** : This means that the treatment or attention given during an emergency situation is not final and may need to be followed with medical up professional treatment. 

**Differences between First Aid AND Medical Treatment** 

- First Aid does not equal medical treatment due to the following reasons: 

 First Aid materials are inadequate or improvised, while medical materials are adequate and available  Medical diagnosis is thorough and involves medical equipment, while diagnosis in First Aid is based on observation 

2 

- il  Medical personnel undergo more intensive training than First Aid personnel 

- Medical treatment may involve many medical professionals, while there are no different professionals in First Aid treatment 

   - In most cases, medical treatment is ultimate while First Aid is not 

   - The following terms are commonly used interchangeably to describe people involved in a health emergency or sudden illness. 

###### **Terms Commonly used to Describe People involved in Emergency Situations** 

- **Casualty** - a person involved in any form of health emergency or accident 

- **Victim** – a person who sustained injury in any health emergency or accident 

- **Patient** – a person who continues to suffer from the injury sustained in any health emergency or accident
