# WRITING 

## 1. Meaning of Writing 

Writing is a productive skill and also a literacy skill. It involves the organisation of thoughts or ideas in a logical and coherent manner, using the type of language that is appropriate for the subject matter, audience, situation and the goal of writing. 

## 2. Relevance of Writing 

The modern man relies on basic skills for survival. Writing guides us to note: 



What we listen to. 





What we say. What we read. 

Writing is an important means of 

communication and helps us express our thoughts, ideas and information clearly. 

3. Principles Underlying Writing Skills There are important principles that guide effective writing. They include: 

### A. Problem 

Writers should identify a problem and see the **need for a solution.** 

### B. Purpose 

A writer must define the purpose of writing. This gives direction and focus to the work. The purpose of writing could be: 

1. To tell a sequence of events. 

2. To explain how something is made. 

3. To explain the reason for something. 

4. To compare or to seek advice. 

The purpose of writing helps determine the 

appropriate grammatical structure and vocabulary to use. 

### C. Audience 

Identifying an audience is important in writing. This makes writing meaningful. It also guides the selection of appropriate language, knowing the potential readers and what they would reasonably expect. It helps the writer decide how to make the writing clear, unambiguous and readable. Examples of audiences include: 









Friends. Sisters or brothers. An executive. The general public. 

D. Experience 

Since no meaningful writing can take place in an **experiential vacuum, the topic must relate to the** writer's experience. 

This means that writers should draw from their knowledge, observations and personal experiences when writing. 

4. Techniques of Writing The following techniques should be considered when writing: 

1. Pay attention to punctuation and other graphological features. Examples of punctuation marks include: 













Comma (,) Full stop (.) Question mark (?) Colon (:) Semicolon (;) Exclamation mark (!) 

2. Correct spelling Writers should ensure that words are spelt correctly to make their work clear and 

understandable
