# Faculty of Education - Study Map

*How the Education notes on Project-NODE connect.*

---

## The courses and how they relate

| Course | What it gives you | Connects to |
|---|---|---|
| **GNS 111 Communication in English** | Word classes, grammar, sentences, writing, critical thinking, public speaking, report writing | Every other course: you write essays and reports in all of them |
| **EDM 111 Introduction to Educational Management** | Management and administration of schools (planning, organising, directing, controlling) | LAS 321 (administrators and accreditation bodies), HKE 210 (safety management in schools) |
| **LAS 321 Curriculum Development** (300 Level) | Objectives, content, evaluation, curriculum models, philosophy of education | EDM 111 (who runs and approves the curriculum), GNS 111 (writing behavioural objectives) |
| **HKE 210 First Aid** (200 Level) | Emergency response and injury management | EDM 111 (a school's duty of care), LAS 321 (health and safety education in the curriculum) |

## Ideas that appear in more than one course

- **Planning, organising, directing, controlling** (EDM 111) is the same cycle as **objectives, content, method, evaluation** (LAS 321). Both are a loop: plan, act, check, improve.
- **Evaluation** appears in EDM 111 (controlling through evaluation and reports) and LAS 321 (formative and summative evaluation).
- **Administrators, accreditation bodies and ministries** are in both EDM 111 and LAS 321 (NUC, NCCE, NERDC, WAEC).
- **Stating objectives with action verbs** (LAS 321) is a writing skill from GNS 111: use specific verbs, one idea per sentence, write in clear sentence patterns.
- **Critical thinking** (GNS 111) helps you compare curriculum models and philosophies (idealism, realism, pragmatism) in LAS 321 and judge arguments in essays.
- **Report writing** (GNS 111) is how the results of school evaluation and administration are communicated (EDM 111).
- **Educational management and the curriculum:** management decides resources and policy; curriculum decides what is taught. Neither works without the other.

## Suggested study order

1. GNS 111 (communication tools for all your other courses)
2. EDM 111 (how schools are run)
3. HKE 210 (practical safety and first aid)
4. LAS 321 (how a curriculum is designed, delivered and evaluated)
